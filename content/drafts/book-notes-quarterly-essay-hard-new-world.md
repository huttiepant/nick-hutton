# Quarterly Essay: Hard New World

## Summary

For decades and decades, Australia has assumed the US is our key ally. This made sense in the unipolar world of the 1990s and early 2000s. America was the great power and had a strong interest in protecting that mantle. But that world has changed. Yes, Donald Trump has done this brazenly and offensively, but it was happening with or without him. China is the power of its own region and the US is happier being great again—all by itself.

But for some reason, Australian politicians do not see this and refuse to align with nations that might offend the US. The fact of the matter is, in the event of a China–US war, there is no guarantee that the US would support us if we were pulled into it.

The other rhetoric that is tiring in Australia is that China is evil—all evil. Yes, there are many things to disagree with in how China operates. But the fact of the matter is, we can live alongside them in harmony, all the while shoring up our own independence. When we blindly follow the US into stupid deals like AUKUS, we are telling China we want to oppose them.

This isn't about taking sides; it's actually the opposite. But the one big step we need to take is not aligning so closely with the US. We have to realise that they don't have our back like they used to and we shouldn't assume they do. This false hope is particularly dangerous as we enter a time that could well be defined by more and more conflict.
