# Getting Things Done

Ben Thompson (of Stratechery fame) said that this is the book that influenced how he works, and from the outside, he's the most productive person I've ever seen. So if I do this, then I'll be the most productive person in the world?

I think Ben will still hold the gold medal for that, but this is definitely a new way of operating I plan to implement. It goes against most productivity methods that talk about starting by defining your purpose or vision, and its first step is to write down every, single thing in your mind. Once you can put all your mind into a system that you know you'll return to (that's the other tricky part), then you'll be more free and open to explore things higher up - like your purpose and mission etc.

I like the idea and have already started implementing it into life. I feel more free, I feel more confident that I can just do things when they come up and I feel like I won't have more organisational blunders in my life.

The trick to any book like this is to check back in in a year and see how it's going.
