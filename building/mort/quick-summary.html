Summary

I admire people who think clearly and deeply. People like Patrick Collison and Tyler Cowen seem to share a common trait which is they read particularly well. I believe this kind of reading and thinking can be learnt, which is why I'm building Mort.

I'm also particularly interested in building AI agents, how memory works and how we can build better recommendation systems.

Mort, inspired by Mortimer Adler, is a private AI reading and thinking agent I'm building for myself. The goal is for it to learn from the books I read and the notes I take, then help me improve my thinking and get better recommendations.
